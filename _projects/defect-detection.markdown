Internship project at Shenzhen Gongji Technology (Jul – Aug 2025): can MLLMs spot fine-grained defects in
equipment rooms without task-specific training data?

## Work

- Evaluated MLLMs on open-world fine-grained defect detection in **zero-shot** and **few-shot** settings.
- Analysed hallucination risk and domain-transfer robustness for safety-relevant inspection.
- Fine-tuned MLLMs with **LoRA** using MS-SWIFT; deployed with **vLLM** to measure inference feasibility and
  deployment cost while preserving general capabilities.

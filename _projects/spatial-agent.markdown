Ongoing graduate research at the AIGC-DL Lab, University of Macau.

## Question

Given a **3D front view** and a **top-down view** of a room, can an MLLM-based multimodal agent move a single rigid
object from its starting position into a target region?

## Setting

- **Intra-room** and **cross-room** scenarios.
- Perception from complementary viewpoints rather than video streams.
- Evaluation of the agent's spatial grounding, plan feasibility, and failure modes when the target region is only
  partially observable.
